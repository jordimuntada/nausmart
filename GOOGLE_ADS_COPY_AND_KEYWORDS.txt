GOOGLE ADS CHARACTER LIMITS:

SEARCH ADS (RSA):
- Headlines: 30 characters each (provide up to 15)
- Descriptions: 90 characters each (provide up to 4)
- Display URL Path: 15 characters each (2 paths)
- Final URL: No limit

DISPLAY ADS:
- Short Headline: 25 characters
- Long Headline: 90 characters
- Description: 90 characters
- Business Name: 25 characters

EXTENSIONS:
- Sitelink Title: 25 characters
- Sitelink Description: 35 characters per line
- Callout: 25 characters
- Structured Snippet: 25 characters per value

TIPS FOR CHARACTER OPTIMIZATION:
1. Use numbers (€1.120 vs "mil ciento veinte euros")
2. Use symbols where allowed (24/7, m², %)
3. Abbreviate carefully (min vs minutos)
4. Remove unnecessary words (de, en, el)
5. Test different variations
