6. BIDDING STRATEGY
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED APPROACH BY CAMPAIGN:

BRAND CAMPAIGNS:
- Strategy: Target Impression Share (90%+)
- Max CPC: 0.50-1.00€
- Goal: Always appear for brand searches

HIGH-INTENT CAMPAIGNS (Office Rental Location):
- Strategy: Target CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
- Target CPA: 30-50€ per lead
- Start with Manual CPC, transition to automated after 30+ conversions

AWARENESS CAMPAIGNS:
- Strategy: Maximize Clicks
- Budget cap: 15€/day initially
- Review and optimize weekly

SMART BIDDING RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Start with Manual CPC for first 2-4 weeks
2. Collect conversion data (minimum 15-30 conversions)
3. Switch to Target CPA or Maximize Conversions
4. Set realistic CPA targets based on your data

CONVERSION METRICS:
- Conversion Rate - Target: >3%
- Cost Per Lead - Target: <40€
- Lead Quality Score (internal tracking)

BUSINESS METRICS:
- Cost Per Visit Scheduled
- Cost Per Contract Signed
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

QUALITY BENCHMARKS FOR REAL ESTATE/OFFICE:
- CTR Industry Average: 2-3%
- Your Target CTR: 4-6%
- CPC Industry Average: 1-3€
- Your Target CPC: 1-2€
- Conversion Rate Average: 2-4%
- Your Target: 4-6%
